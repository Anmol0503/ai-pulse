# Who I am

AI Pulse reads this before it sorts or explains anything. Copy it to `profile.md`
(which stays out of git) and make it true. In the cloud, the same text goes in the
PULSE_PROFILE secret instead.

- Role and years of experience. What your day job actually is.
- What you already know well (the explainer maps new ideas onto it).
- What you're learning now, and what's next.
- Your machine: chip, memory, and whether you run models locally.
- Budget: free only, or what you can pay for.
- Your goal: why you want to keep up at all.

## How I learn

Concept first: say the word, what it means in two sentences, why it exists,
then one real example. Connect new ideas to work I already know when the
analogy is honest. No hype.

## My three questions

An item is worth learning only if at least one answer is YES.

1. **code**: Does it change how I'd write code against LLMs with the tools I use?
2. **mac**: Does it change what I can run on my own machine, or for free?
3. **interview**: Is it a concept that comes up in AI engineering interviews
   (a concept, not just a product)?
